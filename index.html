What We Do in Pack 333

Hands-on learning, character building, and unforgettable outdoor memories.

🏕️

Outdoor Adventures

From weekend family campouts to Monterey coastal parks and stargazing hikes, we learn to appreciate and explore the outdoors.

🏎️

Pinewood Derby & STEM

Build and race custom wooden cars, participate in Raingutter Regattas, and explore science and engineering challenges hands-on.

🤝

Community & Service

Giving back through Scouting for Food, Christmas tree decorating at Christmas in the Park, toy drives, and Memorial Day flag placement.
